Frequently Asked Questions

Real answers to real questions. No jargon, no surprises.

How much does custom software cost for a small business?

Most small business projects start between $1,500 and $5,000 — a fraction of what agencies charge. We keep costs low because we built our own development framework over 4 years that lets us deliver Fortune 500 quality at small business prices.

Every project starts with a free 20-minute call so we can give you an honest estimate before you commit to anything.

I’m not technical. Can I still work with you?

Absolutely — most of our clients aren’t technical. You don’t need to know anything about code, servers, or databases. Just describe what’s slowing your business down, and we’ll figure out the solution.

We explain everything in plain English, not jargon.

Do you always build custom software, or will you recommend something that already exists?

We recommend whatever actually solves your problem. If a tool like Calendly, QuickBooks, or Shopify already does what you need, we’ll tell you — and help you set it up.

If nothing fits, we build custom. If your existing tools just need to talk to each other, we integrate them. You pay for the right answer, not the most expensive one.

How long does it take to build something?

Most clients have something working in their hands within days, not weeks. A professional website typically takes 1–2 weeks. Custom tools and automations vary, but we always start with the single highest-value improvement and deliver it fast.

What if I just need a website?

We build professional, SEO-optimized websites for small businesses — the kind that actually show up in Google and convert visitors into customers. Our sites include mobile-responsive design, structured data for search engines, fast load times, and accessibility features.

No templates, no page builders — custom-built for your business.

What does “SEO” mean and why should I care?

SEO (Search Engine Optimization) means making your website show up when people search Google for what you offer. If someone searches “plumber near me” or “best dentist in Sacramento” and your business doesn’t appear, you’re losing customers to competitors who do.

We bake SEO into every site from day one — it’s not an add-on, it’s how we build.

Can you help automate parts of my business?

Yes — if your team is doing something by hand that follows a pattern (data entry, sending emails, generating reports, scheduling), we can probably automate it. Automation saves you hours every week and eliminates human error.

How is this different from hiring a big agency?

Agencies typically charge $5,000–$15,000+ for a basic website and $3,000–$8,000 per month for SEO. You get account managers, not engineers.

With us, you work directly with the person building your solution — no middlemen, no markups, no overhead. Same (or better) quality at a fraction of the price.

Do you offer ongoing support after the project is done?

Yes. We offer monthly retainers for ongoing maintenance, SEO optimization, content updates, and new feature development. Most clients choose a retainer because their business keeps evolving and they want a technology partner who knows their system inside and out.

Retainers are flexible — scale up or down as needed.

What if I have an idea for an app but don’t know where to start?

That’s exactly where we shine. We’ll build a working proof-of-concept (MVP) so you can test your idea before investing heavily. Most MVPs take days to weeks, not months. You get something real — not just slides and promises.

What industries do you work with?

Any small business that has a problem software can solve. Past and current work includes healthcare (HIPAA-compliant systems), professional services, e-commerce, veteran services, and local service businesses.

Not sure if we can help? The 20-minute call is free.

Why are you so much cheaper than other developers?

Three reasons:

  1. Proprietary framework — We built tools over 4 years that let us build in days what takes others weeks.
  2. AI-augmented workflow — AI handles repetitive engineering tasks so we spend time on what matters — your problem.
  3. Lean operation — No office, no sales team, no overhead. The savings go directly to you.
Do I own the code you build for me?

Yes. When you pay for custom development, you own the deliverables. Your website, your tools, your data — it’s all yours. We don’t hold your business hostage with proprietary lock-in.
