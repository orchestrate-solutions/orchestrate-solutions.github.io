But more than that, we believe every small business deserves access to real software solutions. When you succeed, you come back — and you tell others. Low overhead, fast delivery, same quality. We pass the savings to you.

Are these fixed prices or estimates?

The prices shown are starting points for typical projects. After a free 20-minute call, we provide a fixed quote for your specific needs. No surprise invoices, no scope creep charges.

What if I don’t know which service I need?

That’s exactly what the free consultation is for. Describe your problem and we’ll recommend the right approach — even if it means pointing you to a free tool that already exists.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes. Most projects are split 50/50: half to start, half on delivery. For larger projects, we arrange milestone-based billing so you only pay as value is delivered.

Do I own the code you write?

Yes. Everything we build for you is yours. Full source code, documentation, deployment credentials. If you ever want to take it to another developer, you can.
