Common Questions

How does pricing work?

Every project gets a custom estimate based on the actual work involved. We scope it individually and work within your budget. There’s always a conversation before a number — and that number is always negotiable.

What if I can’t afford the full cost upfront?

We offer payment plans. We can split payments across milestones, spread costs over time, or start with a smaller scope and grow from there. The goal is to get you started, not to turn you away.

How long does a typical project take?

Landing pages can be done in days. Business websites typically take 2–4 weeks. Custom applications may take 4–8 weeks depending on complexity. We’ll give you a clear timeline upfront.

What if I need something not listed?

We handle a wide range of software projects. If you need it built, we can probably build it. Contact us and we’ll scope exactly what you need — the consultation is free.

Do I own the code?

Yes. You own everything we build for you. No vendor lock-in, no proprietary traps. Your business, your code.

What if my project changes scope mid-build?

It happens. We’ll discuss the impact honestly and adjust the estimate. No surprise invoices — we talk through every change before the work happens.

I’m just starting out and don’t know what I need yet.

That’s exactly what the free consultation is for. We’ll help you figure out what makes sense for where you are right now, not sell you things you don’t need yet.

Do you work with non-profits or community organizations?

Yes, and we offer reduced rates for organizations doing good work in their communities. Reach out and let’s talk about it.
