Why this exists

Built because it had to be.

I can’t use my hands the way most developers can. reWASD was the gold standard for controller remapping — but it only runs on Windows. I needed it on macOS. No one had built it. So I did.

This tool is something I use every single day to work. That means every rough edge gets smoothed, every bug matters, and accessibility isn’t a feature — it’s the whole point. What started as a personal necessity is now free for anyone who needs it.

— Joshua Wink, Founder & Creator
